SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT - FOOD SERVICES DELIVERY

1. AGREEMENT OVERVIEW
This Service Level Agreement (SLA) is made between [Vendor Name] ("Service Provider") and [Client Name] ("Client") for food delivery services.

2. FOOD SAFETY STANDARDS

2.1 Temperature Control
- Hot Food: >63°C
- Cold Food: <5°C
- Frozen Items: -18°C or below
- Maximum Temperature Deviation: ±2°C

2.2 Time-Temperature Controls
- Maximum Transit Time: 45 minutes
- Temperature Check: Every 15 minutes
- Temperature Recovery: <10 minutes
- Hot Hold Duration: <2 hours

2.3 Hygiene Standards
- Minimum Hygiene Score: 95/100
- FSSAI Compliance: Mandatory
- Daily Sanitation Check
- Monthly Audit

3.1 Time Standards

3.2 Quality Metrics
