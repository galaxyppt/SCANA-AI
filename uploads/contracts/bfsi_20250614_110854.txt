RETAIL SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

1. DELIVERY TIMING
   The Supplier shall ensure all retail deliveries are made between 07:00-21:00 hours on all days.
   Standard deliveries must be completed within 2 days of order placement.
   Express deliveries must be completed within 24 hours of order placement.
   Peak season (Nov-Dec) deliveries may have a 1-day extension with prior notification.

2. ORDER PROCESSING
   2.1 Order Confirmation
   - Orders must be confirmed within 15 minutes of receipt
   - Inventory availability must be verified before confirmation
   - Any substitutions must be approved by the retailer
   
   2.2 Order Fulfillment
   - Orders must be picked and packed within 2 hours of confirmation
   - Order accuracy must be 99.5% or higher
   - Barcode verification required for all items

3. QUALITY STANDARDS
   3.1 Product Quality
   - All products must be in saleable condition
   - Minimum 80% of shelf life remaining for perishable goods
   - No visible damage to packaging allowed
   
   3.2 Stock Accuracy
   - Inventory accuracy must be maintained at 98% or higher
   - Real-time inventory updates required
   - Discrepancies must be reported within 4 hours

4. RETURN PROCESSING
   4.1 Return Authorization
   - Return requests must be processed within 24 hours
   - Return labels must be provided electronically
   - Return reason codes must be accurately captured
   
   4.2 Refund Processing
   - Refunds must be processed within 48 hours of return receipt
   - Condition assessment must be completed within 24 hours
   - Detailed return reports provided weekly

5. COMPLIANCE
   5.1 Monitoring
   - All deliveries will be monitored for compliance
   - Weekly performance reports will be generated
   - Monthly business reviews will be conducted
   
   5.2 Penalties
   - Late deliveries: 5% penalty per day beyond agreed time
   - Order accuracy below 99.5%: 2% penalty on invoice value
   - Stock accuracy below 98%: 3% penalty on monthly invoice
   - Late return processing: $10 per day per return
   - Repeated violations may lead to contract termination
