DELIVERY SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

1. DELIVERY TIMING
   The Supplier shall ensure all deliveries are made between 08:00-17:00 hours on business days.
   Emergency deliveries must be completed within 4 hours of order placement.

2. TEMPERATURE REQUIREMENTS
   2.1 Cold Chain Products
   - Temperature must be maintained between 2°C to 8°C during transport
   - Temperature deviation tolerance: ±1°C for no more than 30 minutes
   
   2.2 Frozen Products
   - Must be kept below -18°C throughout transport
   - No temporary temperature excursions allowed

3. QUALITY STANDARDS
   3.1 Product Quality
   - Minimum quality score of 85 points required for acceptance
   - Products scoring between 80-85 will be flagged for review
   - Products scoring below 80 will be rejected

4. DOCUMENTATION
   4.1 Temperature Logs
   - Continuous temperature monitoring required
   - Logs must be provided upon delivery
   
   4.2 Quality Assessment
   - Quality inspection must be performed at pickup and delivery
   - Assessment forms must be completed and signed

5. COMPLIANCE
   5.1 Monitoring
   - All deliveries will be monitored for compliance
   - Monthly performance reports will be generated
   
   5.2 Penalties
   - Non-compliance may result in financial penalties
   - Repeated violations may lead to contract termination
